Privacy Policy

Pigs Gonna Blow! (Cheeseburger Dragon vs. The Exploding Pig) — last updated 2026-09-15

This game is a small, independently-run project. This page explains what information it collects, why, and who it's shared with — in plain language, not legal boilerplate.

What we collect

  • If you sign in: your email address, used only to send you a passwordless "magic link" sign-in email and to identify your account. We never see or store a password.
  • Your display name and chosen avatar/skin, so they can show up next to your scores on the leaderboard.
  • Gameplay scores you submit. The leaderboard is public — anyone can see the name and score you submit, so don't submit anything you don't want publicly visible.
  • Purchases: if you buy a cosmetic skin, the payment itself is handled entirely by Stripe on their own checkout page. We never see or store your card details — we only receive confirmation that a purchase succeeded and which item to grant you.
  • Local device storage: some in-game preferences may be saved in your browser's local storage. That data stays on your device and isn't sent to us.

What we don't do

  • No advertising trackers, ad networks, or third-party analytics pixels.
  • We don't sell or rent your information to anyone.
  • We don't use your email for anything other than sign-in — no marketing emails.

Who we share data with

We use two third-party services to run the game, and your data is only ever shared with them as needed to provide the service:

  • Supabase — hosts our database, handles sign-in, and stores account/profile/score data.
  • Stripe — processes skin purchases. Stripe's own privacy policy governs any payment information you give them directly.

Children's privacy

This game isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us at the address below and we'll remove it.

Your choices

You can play the entire game without ever signing in or submitting a score — an account is only needed to save a personal best or buy a skin. If you'd like your account, profile, or score data deleted, email us and we'll take care of it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions, data deletion requests, or anything else: support@pigsgonnablow.com
